Our Commitment to Accuracy

We take accuracy seriously. Our calculator uses official NEC data tables and formulas to ensure reliable results:

  • NEC Chapter 9, Table 1: Maximum conduit fill percentages (53% for 1 conductor, 31% for 2 conductors, 40% for 3+ conductors)
  • NEC Chapter 9, Table 4: Internal conduit dimensions and cross-sectional areas
  • NEC Chapter 9, Table 5: Wire dimensions including insulation for various conductor types
  • NEC Article 310.15(C)(1): Ampacity derating factors for bundled conductors

⚠️ Important Disclaimer: While we strive for accuracy, this calculator is intended as a planning tool. Always consult with a licensed electrician and verify compliance with local electrical codes before performing electrical work.
